# FunBeat

A DIY desk companion that brings your workspace to life.

FunBeat is a personal hobby project built around a small desktop enclosure
with a TFT display, control knob, and buttons.

The idea is simple: when things get chaotic on the screen and you find
yourself stuck, overwhelmed, or staring blankly at your workspace, FunBeat
gives your setup a little presence and something to interact with.

It isn't meant to solve a major problem or be another productivity tool.
It's simply meant to make your workspace feel alive — providing feedback,
ambient information, and small interactions that can help you reset,
refocus, and get moving again.
